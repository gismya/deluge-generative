# 13. Perform

Part of the [Generative mode spec](../spec.md). Section numbers are shared across all files.

### 13.1 Its own song

- Perform builds its own `Song` from the Current Recipe, with one Output per part. It never layers onto the player's song.
- Entry ([§14.3](14-entry-and-compose-screen.md#143-replacing-the-song)) builds it privately while the current song keeps playing, swaps it in **stopped**, and opens on the SONG page, waiting for PLAY.
- Steering edits the Current Recipe itself, so the Recipe is always the current centre. Arc state, mutes, solos, holds, freezes and focus are **performance state**: never stored, and lost on stop or exit. A restart ([§13.2](#132-transport-start-and-end)) loses them too, except focus.
- A piece starts from PLAY, and from a **restart**: the same Recipe from bar 1. PLAY and a restart AS IT IS read the Current Recipe as the player left it, with the same seed, takes, sound picks and homes, so with no steering the Generator plays the same notes again. A restart AS IT BEGAN goes back to the piece's start state first ([§13.2](#132-transport-start-and-end)), so it plays the first run's notes again whatever was steered since.
- A sound-control home is the param's value on the part's Output, so it lasts until that Output is replaced (genre change, Regenerate, LOAD, sound re-roll). Homes are never saved in a Recipe file.
- The normal song, clip and arranger views can't be reached from Perform.

### 13.2 Transport, Start and End

- PLAY is the only start and stop. There's no separate "Generator on".
- **Start.** PLAY from stop plays the genre's **Intro**, one phrase by default ([§9.3](09-arc.md#93-section-lengths)), with Rise across it, then Groove at the next phrase line (Reset on its downbeat). SHIFT+PLAY skips the Intro and starts in Groove. Every start is fresh: the Arc resets and a new piece begins.
- **Stop.** PLAY stops at once, the Deluge way: notes released, tails ring out. Every queued action is dropped, a key change included, so the next start plays the key the last piece played ([§2.6](02-architecture.md#26-steering-into-the-core)).
- **End** queues the genre's **Outro** from the next phrase line, one phrase by default ([§9.3](09-arc.md#93-section-lengths)), with Close and Wash across it. Energy and Density fall and parts leave through their gates. The transport stops on the phrase line where the Outro ends. End is armed by holding its pad 0.6 s, and a tap cancels it while queued. Once the Outro plays, End does nothing. A Section forced to start on the line where the Outro ends cancels the ending: the transport keeps going in the forced Section.
- **Restart AS IT IS.** X+PLAY, or RESTART ▸ AS IT IS in the Perform menu ([§13.6](#136-perform-menu)), with a RESTART popup. While playing, it queues to the **next bar line**: it shows in QUEUE as RESTART (`RST` in the status line) and on the SONG page's Section row, and BACK, or X+PLAY again (RESTART CANCELLED), cancels it until its line's step is decided, 12 ticks before the line ([§12.2](12-runtime.md#122-when-steps-are-decided)); from there it can't be cancelled, a second X+PLAY does nothing, and a press made then goes with the queue as the new piece starts, as at any start. On that line every note still sounding ends, as on a Section line (a hit leaning past the line goes, as at a stop), and the piece starts again at bar 1 **with the Intro**, as PLAY from stop does, from the Current Recipe as it is then, with a take or octave shift that landed on the line. The transport doesn't stop: under an external clock the clock keeps running, and the new piece's bar 1 is the master's bar line. Mutes, solos, forces, the Arc hold, freezes, a waiting End and every other queued action are cleared; sounds waiting for their line are dropped, and a sound still preloading lands at the new piece's next phrase line once loaded, as after a stop. Nothing else carries over from the first run (Arc counters, fill and drop-out draws, Groove lengths). Focus stays. While stopped, X+PLAY is PLAY. Refused with CHANGE PENDING while a transition is pending ([§12.9](12-runtime.md#129-instances-and-the-transition)), and a genre change, Regenerate or LOAD is refused the same way while a restart waits.
- **Restart AS IT BEGAN.** RESTART ▸ AS IT BEGAN in the Perform menu. As AS IT IS, but from the piece's **start state**, its header in the steering log ([§17.2](17-capture.md#172-the-steering-log)): the Recipe as the piece began (macros, octave shifts, Part biases, key, tempo, swing and swing interval), its seed and takes, its sound picks, and its param snapshot, so every home and player edit goes back too. So after steering (macros, a take, a re-roll) it plays the first run's notes again. How the piece opened isn't restored: it starts from bar 1 with the Intro, as AS IT IS does, even for a piece that opened in Groove (SHIFT+PLAY, a hard cut) or on a Drop, and the Arc hold isn't carried. "The piece" is the one playing, so after a restart it is the restarted one. Parts whose picks changed since (a re-roll, back to the previous sound, a chosen preset) preload the header's picks first, one after another under the working animation, BUSY if the runner is busy; so does a part whose re-roll waits for its phrase line, which may come before the restart's; the restart then queues to the next bar line as AS IT IS does (RESTART popup, RST), so it lands on the first bar line after they have loaded. Until then a restart is ahead (a transition is refused), and BACK, AS IT BEGAN again or X+PLAY cancel the preload, playing or stopped (RESTART CANCELLED); a press of either while the restart waits cancels it. From the press to the restart's line, sound re-rolls, back to the previous sound and chosen sounds are refused with BUSY: one landing first would outlive the restart. On the line the preloaded Outputs swap in (each part's old sound is its previous one, and retires as a re-roll's does), the snapshot is written back over every Output, and the Current Recipe becomes the header's: a save or a Compose after it reads the start state, and the takes made stay. A piece whose header the log couldn't keep ([§17.2](17-capture.md#172-the-steering-log)) restarts AS IT IS. While stopped, it restores the last piece's start state (its sounds first) and starts, as PLAY does; when the last piece isn't the Current Recipe's (a Regenerate, genre change or LOAD since, or nothing played yet), it is PLAY. PLAY while its sounds preload stopped cancels it, as it does a transition's wait.

### 13.3 Perform settings

- **Glide**: how long tempo and swing glides take. Instant, 1 beat, **1 bar** (default), 2 bars, 4 bars or 1 phrase. It is a Perform setting, not a Recipe value, kept until power-off, set in the Perform menu's GLIDE ([§13.6](#136-perform-menu)). A transition's Build or Outro sets its own glide length, and a hard cut uses this one ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)).
- **Browser preview**: whether the preset and lane browsers ([§13.5](#135-control-layout)) play the file under the cursor. Off, **When stopped** (default) or Always. A Perform setting, kept until power-off, set in the Perform menu's BROWSER PREVIEW ([§13.6](#136-perform-menu)). A lane browser's sample previews through the stock preview voice at the lane's volume (its row's and its Kit's, and the song's), as stock's sample browser previews at a kit row's. Presets don't preview yet: the setting reaches the lane browser's samples only (#300).
- **Transition style** is chosen per change in the confirm ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)), not stored.

### 13.4 Live actions

| Action | Grain |
|---|---|
| Macros | next undecided step ([§3.4](03-recipe.md#34-steering-grain)) |
| Tempo, swing | glide from now |
| Key, scale | next phrase line, or an Arc Drop if one lands first ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)) |
| Genre, Regenerate, Recipe LOAD | transition ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)) |
| Force a Section, hold | next phrase line / next undecided step |
| End | next phrase line |
| Restart AS IT IS (X+PLAY) | next bar line ([§13.2](#132-transport-start-and-end)) |
| Restart AS IT BEGAN | preload of the changed sounds, then next bar line ([§13.2](#132-transport-start-and-end)) |
| Part mute and solo | next bar. SHIFT = next undecided step |
| Part octave shift | next bar |
| Sound re-roll; back to previous sound | preload, then next phrase line |
| Pattern re-roll (new take); step back or forward through takes | next phrase line |
| Freeze / unfreeze | next undecided step |
| Fill | while held |
| Capture mark | now |
| Sound-control home edits | now, logged at the next undecided step |

- "Now" in this table means the next undecided step, because every action reaches the core at a decision ([§2.6](02-architecture.md#26-steering-into-the-core)). Only Capture marks, home edits and tempo and swing glides, which never enter the core, act at once.
- A muted part keeps generating ([§12.5](12-runtime.md#125-mute-gate-and-note-offs)). Unmuting a part a Break has silenced brings it back only when the Arc does.
- "Back to previous sound" remembers one previous pick per part, as performance state.
- While a transition is pending, fact edits, arming End, take and sound actions are refused, and the new roster starts clean ([§12.9](12-runtime.md#129-instances-and-the-transition)). An octave shift isn't refused: it lands at the outgoing piece's next bar line, and the incoming Recipe brings its own shifts (all 0 after a genre change or Regenerate, a new Recipe's).

### 13.5 Control layout

Stock habits come first: a control does what its printed label or stock behaviour suggests wherever Perform has a matching action. The prototype on [`prototype/perform-control-layout`](https://github.com/gismya/deluge-generative/tree/prototype/perform-control-layout) at `111e4968` is the visual reference.

**Grid pages.** SONG, CLIP and KEYBOARD switch the grid page, and their LEDs show which is on. KEYBOARD on the KEYBOARD page flips to stock Performance view and back, with KEYBOARD's LED blinking there. The sidebar is the same on every page.

- **SONG, the Arc map.** One column per phrase. Column 4 is now; columns to its left have played and columns to its right are the Arc's plan.
  - Rows 1–6, parts: bright = sounded, the now column flashes live, dim = planned to sound. A part sounded in a phrase when a note of it rang there: one started in it, or one held on over its line, as a Drone or Pad holds its voicing. A muted note doesn't count. Any note counts at every step it sounds, so a long gate held a few ticks over a line lights the part in the next phrase too, even where the plan showed it silent. Holding a part's row focuses it. "Planned to sound" means the Arc lets the part play on some step of the phrase (its Density gate is open and Break or Build keeps don't stop it, or the Build roll plays one of its lanes) and it is audible there (not muted or soloed out). It leaves out the cut and the Gap, which the MUTE pad's dim counts: neither covers a whole phrase (a genre's data check holds each shorter than its phrase). In a planned column, a part muted there shows faint red. The now column flashes a part that played a note in the previous 16th or in this one so far, so a flash can span two 16ths. It is sampled once a 16th, so the flash redraws the pads at most once per 16th.
  - Row 7: effective Energy against the Recipe centre, averaged over the phrase. Blue below, green at (less than 2 points from it), orange above.
  - Row 8: the Section of each phrase, the one playing its last step, so a Build shorter than a phrase shows in the phrase it ends.
  - A queued action blinks in the column where it lands: a mute, solo, take or sound on its part's row, a force, End or key change on row 8. The plan redraws to show its result. Columns after the line where the Outro ends are dark.
  - Turning X scrolls, back over the last 15 phrases the history keeps and ahead to the end of the 16-phrase plan. Tapping X returns to now; a turn or a solo while X is down isn't a tap.
- **CLIP, one part in depth.** It shows the last part held or locked, and choosing this page doesn't move the gold knobs. AFFECT ENTIRE does: it locks the part shown, or unlocks it if it is the one locked (see **Focus** below).
  - Row 1: takes 1–16. Bright = playing, dim = an earlier take, grey = a new take, blinking = queued. Tapping a take queues it. Only the lit pads act: the takes made so far (a queued one counted) and the next new one, which is grey. Tapping the queued take again, or the take that plays, cancels the one queued. While stopped a take acts at once: it is the Recipe's take, and the next start plays it.
  - Row 2: FREEZE, NEW SOUND (SHIFT = previous sound), SOUND EDITOR, on pads 1–3. FREEZE is bright while the part is frozen. NEW SOUND blinks while its sound preloads or waits for the phrase line ([§10.7](10-sounds.md#107-when-sounds-load)), a sound chosen in the preset browser too, and pressing it then cancels that sound. NEW SOUND always rolls from the palette: the browser is SYNTH's. SOUND EDITOR opens the part's sound editor at its root menu ([§13.7](#137-sound-editor-midi-and-external-tempo)).
  - Rows 3–8: Brightness, Thin, Decay, Space, Drive and Wobble as horizontal faders. Tapping a pad sets home. The white pad is the live value, drift included.
  - Each fader shows and sets its control's **lead param** on the part: the first param the control moves there ([§11.2](11-sound-controls.md#112-mapping-per-engine)'s order, among those the preset uses now), such as the LPF for Brightness on a subtractive synth. Its 16 pads span the param's knob range: lit up to home, and the live value's pad white, on home's pad too. A tap sets the param's home ([§11.6](11-sound-controls.md#116-home-and-player-edits-perform)). The control's other params keep their homes; the sound editor reaches them. A row stays dark where the preset doesn't use the control. A Kit part's faders are its kit-wide controls. An FX part has no kit-wide controls (each lane has its own), so its faders stay dark. The faders are read about ten times a second (*tuning*), so a ramp moves the white pad without redrawing the grid every frame.
- **KEYBOARD, performance pads** (stock KEYBOARD opens PerformanceView, so this keeps the habit).
  - Columns 1–8: Energy, Density, Complexity and Motion as faders, two columns each. Tap = new centre. Hold = a temporary value that springs back on release. White = where the Arc has the value now.
  - A fader's 8 rows span 0–50, the bottom row 0 and the top 50: row *n* from the bottom (0–7) is *n* × 50 / 7 points, to the nearest. A press steers the core with its row's value at once (the next undecided step, [§13.4](#134-live-actions)), whether it turns out a tap or a hold, as stock's PerformanceView pads do. Let go within 300 ms it is a tap, and the value stays as the Recipe's centre. Held longer, the release steers the core back to the centre. Either way each value is logged where it lands, so a hold logs two entries. The 300 ms is fixed, the user's choice after trying stock's Hold Press Time (100 ms by default) on the Deluge and finding it too short; the faders never read that setting, so stock's own pads keep it.
  - A second pad on a held fader moves the held value; the hold ends with its last pad.
  - A piece or a new instance that starts while a fader is held (PLAY, or a Regenerate or genre change landing) plays the held value from its start, as a held FILL does ([§8.7](08-rhythm-and-expression.md#87-fill)); the release springs back to the centre. A tap that spans a start leaves its value as the centre in the Recipe and in the new piece. A gold-knob turn on a held macro moves only its centre (the rings and popup show it): the core keeps the held value, and the release springs back to the new centre. A tap's value wins over a knob turn made during it.
  - The pads are lit up to the value in effect (the held value while held, brighter), and while playing the pad where the Arc has the value is white, on a lit pad too. Complexity and Motion's white pad is their value, since the Arc never moves them ([§3.3](03-recipe.md#33-macros)).
  - Column 14, rows 1–4: phrase position, a quarter of the phrase per pad from the top, in the playing Section's colour: played quarters dim, the one playing bright, the rest faint. Dark while stopped.
  - Columns 14–16, rows 5–8: FILL, held. SHIFT = a fresh pick ([§8.7](08-rhythm-and-expression.md#87-fill)). Any of the 12 pads and SYNC-SCALING hold FILL together: it starts with the first held, with SHIFT as that one went down, and ends with the last let go. The pads are white while FILL is on, from any source (MIDI FILL too), and grey otherwise.
  - A fader or FILL pad whose release an overlay swallowed lets go when Perform is back on top; a fader held past 300 ms springs back then.
  - **Punch-ins** ([§8.9](08-rhythm-and-expression.md#89-punch-ins)), on the free pads. Columns 9–10: a lane mute per Drums lane, in the part's lane order, top to bottom down column 9 and on down column 10 (Techno's seven lanes fill column 9's top seven); the rest dark. Columns 15–16, rows 1–3: HAT ROLL and SNARE ROLL, FLAM and DOUBLE KICK, then PUMP. A lane pad is green while its lane plays and red while muted; each other pad has its own colour, faint while off and bright while on. They follow the faders' 300 ms rule: a press turns an off punch-in on at once (the next undecided step); let go within 300 ms it latches, held longer it springs back off. A tap or a hold on a latched one turns it off as it is let go. Several can be on at once, and they stay on across a stop. A full steering queue refuses a change with the queue popup, and the pad stays as it was. A pad whose release an overlay swallowed lets go when Perform is back on top. SHIFT + a punch-in pad is the sound editor, as on any main pad. The layout is a proposal, for the owner to approve on the Deluge. Columns 11–13 stay free.
  - **With a part focused** (the Focus rule below), the faders steer that part's Part bias ([§3.3](03-recipe.md#33-macros)), not the Recipe value. Each fader is lit up to the part's biased value at the centre, and the white pad marks the Recipe centre's row (while playing too, in place of the Arc's). Pressing a row sets the bias that puts the part there: the smallest one whose biased value reaches the row, about 4/3 of row − centre through the middle, as the blend moves a part three quarters of its bias there (from the centre 25 only +50 reaches 50). Pressing the centre's row sets it to 0. Tap and hold follow the 300 ms rule as above: a tap keeps the bias in the Recipe, a hold plays it until let go and springs back to the Recipe's. The press steers the part focused as it went down, whatever is focused at the release. With nothing focused the faders set the Recipe value as above. SHIFT + pad stays the sound editor.

- **Stock Performance view**, KEYBOARD's second page: stock PerformanceView's song FX pads, hosted over Perform. Its layout is stock's (the card's `SETTINGS/PerformanceView.XML` once a layout has been saved in stock's own view, else stock's default: LPF and HPF frequency and resonance, bass, treble, reverb, delay amount and rate, mod FX rate, depth, feedback and offset, decimation, bitcrush and stutter), read the first time the page opens in an entry. Its pads are stock's own: a press sets the column's param to the row's depth at once, a tap latches it and a hold springs back after stock's Hold Press Time, stutter holds only, and the render and colours are stock's. They act on Perform's song, on the whole mix whatever is focused: with a part focused, the knobs move to the song for each press and back after it. Over Perform's screen the value shows as stock's knob popup. Perform's frame runs on around it: the sidebar's mutes, solos, forces and the hold stay live, and so do the gold knobs, PLAY, the menus and the OLED (its MACROS page). A pad pressed there lets go there, on any page, a stutter included. Column editing stays in stock's own view, outside Perform: the layout saved there is the one this page loads. A stock column held on another song before Perform's entry doesn't carry over. Its pads' param edits are logged as song param edits (`SetParam`) and its stutter as `SetStutter`, on and off ([§17.2](17-capture.md#172-the-steering-log)); a gold knob's stutter is logged the same way. Hosting needs no stock edit: Perform calls stock's own `padAction()` and `renderMainPads()` and stays the root UI.

**Sidebar**, the same on every page:

- **MUTE/LAUNCH, rows 1–6:** part mute at the next bar; SHIFT = at the next undecided step. X held + MUTE pad = solo, as stock. Colours are stock: green active, red muted, blue solo, dim = silenced by the Arc or soloed out. While any part is soloed, only soloed parts sound, muted or not; a muted part's mute takes effect again when the last solo goes, and its pad stays red meanwhile. "Silenced by the Arc" means its Density gate is closed or section rules (Break keeps, the cut, the Gap) stop all its notes. A lane the Build roll plays counts as sounding whatever its Density gate, since the roll ignores gates (§8.3): a part whose only sounding lane is rolling isn't dim. A Rest, a Replace pair or a mute doesn't dim a pad.
- **AUDITION/SECTION:** rows 1–3 force Groove, Break or Build (again cancels). Row 5 holds the Arc. Row 8 is End: hold 0.6 s to arm (the pad brightens while held); a tap cancels. Both only with Perform on top: under an overlay (a menu, the sound editor, the preset browser) a hold doesn't arm End and a tap doesn't cancel it. Each force pad and End show their Section's colour, bright while it plays; the hold pad is white while held.
- The sidebar does nothing while stopped: mutes, solos, holds and forces are performance state, and a start is fresh.

**OLED.** Only the stock 7, 9, 13 and 20 px fonts, no 5 px text. Popups are stock style.

- **Status line, always shown.** Left: Section and count (the phrase within it, `n/m` when its length is planned, `n` for a Groove), and HELD (H when the right side leaves no room for it, nothing when it leaves none for H either). Right: the next queued action as an inverted tag with its bar count (`>BR3`, plus `+` when more are queued), or bar.beat when nothing is queued. EXT when following an external clock. A Capture dot at the right end, once a piece has played: filled = the current piece is marked, hollow = it isn't, blinking = log full. It takes two characters, which HELD's room counts. A phrase bar underneath. STOPPED when stopped.
- **The page follows the grid page.** SONG → ARC: now and the next 7 phrases as boxes (queued ones blink, Drop marked) and the Arc's next change, counted down to the step it starts on (a Build shorter than a phrase starts partway into the phrase before its Drop). CLIP → PART: the part's name and a synth part's preset (its file name; a Kit part has no one pick, each lane having its own sample, so it shows its name only), take n/m on the right (`T2/3`: the take playing of the takes made, a queued one counted, with `*` while frozen), and the six controls as bars (home plus a live tick), in two columns in the CLIP page's order, each labelled with its name's first three letters. KEYBOARD → MACROS: four bars, fill = centre, tick = where the Arc has it (while playing). Each row starts with the macro's initial and its centre in points (`E25`).
- **SELECT turn** shows QUEUE (queued actions with their countdowns, soonest first: three lines, so with four or more it shows two and "+N MORE"; a label is cut short to fit beside its countdown in 21 characters), RECIPE or CAPTURE (THIS PIECE: MARKED, NOT MARKED or NONE; MARKED PIECES: the count; LOG: the share used, or FULL) for 4 s. The first turn shows QUEUE to the right and RECIPE to the left; later turns go round RECIPE, QUEUE, CAPTURE, right forward and left back. **SELECT push** opens the Perform menu ([§13.6](#136-perform-menu)).

**Gold knobs.** Pages are stock everywhere, and the F page is remembered per target, as stock does. Perform owns the mod buttons, so pressing F1 again never turns the VU meter on over the mute column. Knob LEDs show the value; for macros, that's the centre.

- **Recipe level** (nothing focused): F8 = Energy / Density. Pressing F8 again switches to Complexity / Motion, and the F8 LED blinks while that set is on. This displaces stock's song-level decimation and bitcrush. F1–F7 stay stock song FX, pushes included (F2 filter type, F7 stutter).
- **Part focused:** F8 = the part's Part bias ([§3.3](03-recipe.md#33-macros)): Energy / Density, and Complexity / Motion on a second press, as at the Recipe level, displacing the part's stock F8 page. A detent moves the bias 25 units; the ring shows the part's biased value at the centre and the popup the value and the bias (`ENERGY 36 +11`). MIDI's macro CCs stay the Recipe level's. F1–F7 are the part's stock Sound pages. Turning a param a control targets sets that control's home: CUTOFF = Brightness, RELEASE = Decay, REVERB and DELAY AMOUNT = Space, MOD DEPTH = Wobble (and MOD RATE where an LFO is patched). DELAY AMOUNT counts only where the preset uses the delay, and on a synth MOD DEPTH (its LFO1 → pitch cable) only where the preset already has that cable ([§11.2](11-sound-controls.md#112-mapping-per-engine)); turning it on a preset without one makes a new cable, which no control moves until the next piece. Pressing F7 again gives Thin / Drive: the upper knob turns Thin's lead param and the lower Drive's (as the CLIP faders do), with the stock popup and knob rings (which stay on Thin and Drive when stock refreshes them, F15 in [§19](19-firmware-changes.md#19-firmware-changes)), and the F7 LED blinks while the set is on. F7 pressed a third time, or any other F button, goes back to stock pages, and focusing another part starts it on stock pages. A sound swap on the part keeps Thin / Drive: the new Output comes up on F7, with its rings and blinking LED. Plain F7 stays stutter / CUSTOM 1, and the upper knob's push stays stutter on either set. Under an overlay Perform opens (the leave menu, the sound editor), the gold knobs and F buttons stay Perform's (F16 in [§19](19-firmware-changes.md#19-firmware-changes)): the macros on F8, Thin / Drive with its rings and blinking LED, and every F press as above, so turning F8 never reaches stock's song-level decimation. Knob pushes stay stock.
- **Focus:** holding a part's row on the SONG page puts it on the knobs while held, like holding a clip. Double-tap (two presses within 0.35 s) locks it, and double-tapping the locked part unlocks it. On the CLIP page, AFFECT ENTIRE flips the knobs between the part shown and the Recipe level: it locks that part (the same lock as a double tap, replacing any other), or unlocks it if it is the one locked, and the page keeps showing it. With no part shown yet it does nothing. On the CLIP page, a row still held from the SONG page stops being held when AFFECT ENTIRE locks or unlocks: letting go of it then changes nothing, and if its part was the one unlocked, the knobs go back to the Recipe level with the finger still down. On the SONG and KEYBOARD pages AFFECT ENTIRE only unlocks. Its LED is lit at Recipe level on every page. A Kit part focuses its whole Kit (its kit-level pages), whichever way it is focused; AFFECT ENTIRE never takes stock's whole kit ↔ one drum meaning. Focus is performance state: a stop or leaving Perform drops it, so a new entry starts with nothing focused. An overlay opening over a held row lets go of the hold (a lock stays). The focused part's pad in the now column is white, which hides its flash.

Plain KIT on a Drums or FX part opens the **lane list** (below); on a synth part it re-rolls |
| CROSS-SCREEN | focused part: freeze (LED = frozen) |
| Y turn / Y push | focused part: take back / forward from the queued take, or else the one playing (forward past the newest makes a new take) / show key, scale and seed code as a popup (`A MINOR 1A2B3C4`) |
| SHIFT + Y turn | focused pitched part: its octave shift down / up one, from the one queued or else the one playing, with a popup of the part and the new shift as QUEUE names it (`PAD OCT +1`). Turned back to the shift that plays, it cancels the queued one. A turn past the shifts the part takes (−2..+2, fewer where its notes would leave MIDI's range, [§7.3](07-pitch.md#73-register)) is refused with a popup of the shift and LOWEST or HIGHEST (`SUB OCT -1 LOWEST`). Nothing with no part focused, or on Drums or FX |
| SHIFT + main pad | sound editor at the printed param: the focused part's Sound, or song FX with nothing focused ([§13.7](#137-sound-editor-midi-and-external-tempo)). Not on the KEYBOARD page's FILL pads or the CLIP page's NEW SOUND and SOUND EDITOR, whose SHIFT means something already. Pads, PLAY and knobs stay live. BACK closes |
| BACK tap / hold | cancel the newest queued action / open the leave menu ([§13.8](#138-leaving)). A hold whose press closed a menu or the preset browser only closes it |
| SAVE / SHIFT+SAVE | quick save while playing, full save UI when stopped / always the full save UI ([§16.4](16-recipe-files.md#164-save)) |
| LOAD | Recipe load browser ([§16.5](16-recipe-files.md#165-load)) |
| MIDI, CV | reserved for later outputs |
| TRIPLETS | unused. LEARN stays stock |

**Preset browser.** SYNTH on a focused synth part opens the stock preset browser's file list on the card's presets, every one, not just the palette's: SELECT turns, the QWERTY keyboard searches, SELECT on a folder goes into it, and BACK goes up a folder. It opens in the folder of the part's pick with the pick selected (SYNTHS/ when that folder is gone). Its title is "Choose sound".

**Lane list and lane browser.** KIT on a focused Drums or FX part opens the lane list, a stock list titled with the part's name: RE-ROLL ALL first, which re-rolls the whole part as NEW SOUND does and closes the list, then one row per lane the part plays with its pick's name (`KICK 909 Kick`, `NONE` for an empty pick). SELECT on a lane opens the lane browser: the preset browser's file list over every audio file on the card, titled with the lane's name, opened in the folder of the lane's pick with it selected (the lane's first palette folder when that is gone, then SAMPLES/). On an FX lane that takes a preset ([§10.5](10-sounds.md#105-fx-lanes)) SELECT first offers SAMPLE or SYNTH PRESET, and SYNTH PRESET opens the preset browser's list for the lane (in its pick's folder when the pick is a preset, else the folder of its first palette preset, then SYNTHS/). BACK in a browser's top folder goes back to the list, and BACK there closes it. The lane list opens, and a lane's browser, only when the preset browser would; KIT while the part's sound preloads or waits cancels that sound instead.

What follows holds for both browsers.

- Scrolling loads nothing into the part: it plays on with its sound. With BROWSER PREVIEW on ([§13.3](#133-perform-settings)), the lane browser plays the sample under the cursor.
- SELECT on a file closes the browser (and the lane list under it) and chooses it: it preloads, swaps in at the next phrase line (as soon as it has loaded while stopped) and cancels as a re-roll does ([§10.7](10-sounds.md#107-when-sounds-load)). A file whose path is longer than a pick holds is refused with PATH TOO LONG, and one chosen while the runner is busy (freeing a swapped-out sound, say) or a transition is pending with a re-roll's popup: either way the browser stays open, to choose again.
- BACK in the top folder (or a BACK hold) closes it with nothing changed.
- It doesn't open while a transition is pending or the runner is busy, with a re-roll's popups. SYNTH while the part's sound preloads or waits cancels that sound instead.
- Underneath, Perform plays on and its frame runs: queued actions land, sounds swap and transitions go on. The sidebar stays Perform's and live for mutes, solos, forces and the hold, and so do PLAY, the gold knobs ([§13.5](#135-control-layout)'s knob pages), TEMPO and SYNC-SCALING's fill. End isn't available while the browser is open, as under Perform's other overlays: a hold on its pad doesn't arm it, and a tap doesn't cancel it. SYNTH, KIT, SAVE and LOAD do nothing in it; stock's long SELECT press and favourites aren't there.

**Key and scale editor.** A stock submenu, KEY / SCALE, with two rows, KEY (C to B) and SCALE (the 12 scales, in the short names QUEUE uses), each with its value right-aligned and edited in the stock list. SCALE opens it, and so does KEY / SCALE in the Perform menu.

- It starts from the key ahead: the queued change's key, or else the key that plays. Each turn queues the key and scale it shows to the next phrase line, or an Arc Drop if one lands first ([§13.4](#134-live-actions)), replacing a change already queued. Turning back to the key that plays cancels the queued change.
- While stopped, each turn sets the Recipe's key and scale at once, as a macro turn does, and the next start plays them.
- The SCALE LED blinks from the step decision that takes the change in to the one that lands it, 12 ticks before it is heard ([§12.2](12-runtime.md#122-when-steps-are-decided)).

**Queued actions.** A queued action blinks on its pad and shows in the status line, and QUEUE lists every one. Pressing the action again cancels it, and so does a BACK tap, newest first. Quantised actions close 12 ticks early ([§12.2](12-runtime.md#122-when-steps-are-decided)). A Queued transition shows as `CHG` in the status line and as CHANGE DROP, CHANGE FADE or CHANGE CUT in QUEUE, counting down to where its Build or Outro starts, or to its cut line; it lands on the Section row of the SONG page. While its preload runs it isn't queued yet: the working animation shows it, and a BACK tap cancels it first ([§12.9](12-runtime.md#129-instances-and-the-transition)).

**Pad redraws.** A full pad redraw costs 21–22 ms of UART, shared with the OLED. Redraw only changed rows. Once per 16th uses about 19% of the link at 130 BPM, and the ceiling is about 45 full redraws a second.

### 13.6 Perform menu

Stock look, opened by SELECT push (SHIFT+SELECT stays stock). Rows with a value show it right-aligned, as the Compose screen's do ([§14.4](14-entry-and-compose-screen.md#144-the-compose-screen)):

- CANCEL CHANGE (shown only while a genre change, Regenerate or LOAD can still be cancelled: preloading, or queued before its line)
- GENRE ▸: the genres, grouped as in [§14.1](14-entry-and-compose-screen.md#141-the-generative-menu), the current one labelled `= REGEN` and picking it a Regenerate. Picking another genre is a genre change ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)). In M1 the rows are the Techno subgenres
- REGENERATE
- KEY / SCALE ▸: the key and scale editor ([§13.5](#135-control-layout))
- GLIDE: Instant, 1 beat, 1 bar, 2 bars, 4 bars or 1 phrase ([§13.3](#133-perform-settings)), at once
- SEED + TAKES ▸: titled with the seed code (`SEED 1A2B3C4`), one row per part with its take (1–16), the queued one or else the one playing. A turn steps from the take it shows as a Y turn does ([§13.5](#135-control-layout)): forward past the newest makes a new take, and back onto the take that plays cancels the queued one. NEW SEED is the Compose screen's only: a new seed mid-piece would also move the Arc's plan.
- OCTAVES ▸: one row per pitched part (Drums and FX have none) with its octave shift (shown `+1`), the queued one or else the one playing. A row offers only the shifts its part takes ([§7.3](07-pitch.md#73-register)). A turn steps from the shift it shows as a SHIFT + Y turn does ([§13.5](#135-control-layout)), past the end with the same LOWEST or HIGHEST popup; back onto the shift that plays cancels the queued one. Stopped, the shift is the Recipe's at once.
- RESET PART BIAS: the focused part's Part bias on every macro back to 0, or every part's with nothing focused ([§3.3](03-recipe.md#33-macros)). It acts at once and closes the menu.
- RESTART ▸: AS IT IS, the restart X+PLAY makes, and AS IT BEGAN, from the piece's start state ([§13.2](#132-transport-start-and-end)). The menu closes, so its popup, the working animation and the queue show on the Perform page.
- CAPTURE ▸: the pieces in the steering log, newest first (`2 MELODIC TECHNO`, MARKED or `-`), a press toggling one's mark; NOTHING PLAYED before any piece ([§17.4](17-capture.md#174-marking)). The menu stays open.
- BROWSER PREVIEW: Off, When stopped or Always ([§13.3](#133-perform-settings)), at once

Under the menu the playback and the gold knobs stay Perform's (F16), with their rings and blinking F7 and F8 LEDs. A pad press closes it. SHIFT + a main pad opens the sound editor there instead, as from Perform ([§13.7](#137-sound-editor-midi-and-external-tempo)).

Genre, Regenerate and a mid-set LOAD confirm with a choice: BUILD INTO A DROP, FADE THROUGH or HARD CUT AT PHRASE ([§9.7](09-arc.md#97-transitions)), in a stock context menu titled REGENERATE? for a Regenerate, and for a genre change with the incoming genre's full name and a question mark (`MELODIC TECHNO?`; OLED names are at most 14 characters, [§4.4](04-genres.md#44-names-and-versions), so it always fits). The incoming genre's transition entry is highlighted. A choice closes the menu back to the Perform page, which shows the working animation while the incoming roster loads; one refused (Committed or Retiring, [§12.9](12-runtime.md#129-instances-and-the-transition)) shows CHANGE PENDING. CANCEL CHANGE also closes the menu.

### 13.7 Sound editor, MIDI and external tempo

**Sound editor overlay.** A part's normal sound editor opens inside Perform while it keeps playing. It needs F6 and F7.

- SHIFT + a main pad opens it at the printed param: on the focused part's Sound (a Kit part's kit-level FX, as on the gold knobs), or on the song FX with nothing focused. SOUND EDITOR on the CLIP page opens the shown part's root menu. The pads whose SHIFT already means something keep it: the KEYBOARD page's FILL pads (a fresh pick), and the CLIP page's NEW SOUND (the previous sound) and SOUND EDITOR.
- Pads, PLAY, SYNC-SCALING (FILL while held, whatever the community setting) and knobs stay live with no grey-out. Every pad press reaches Perform: holding a part's row focuses it as it does without the editor, and SHIFT + a main pad moves the editor to that param, on whatever is focused then. The gold knobs stay where focus puts them (F16), so they can be on another part than the editor.
- Knob moves and edits in the editor set homes and are logged ([§11.6](11-sound-controls.md#116-home-and-player-edits-perform)). MIDI feedback goes out only for what the gold knobs point at, as below.
- Perform stays the root UI and never becomes a ClipMinder (F6): SAVE preset, KEYBOARD, CLIP (automation) and AFFECT ENTIRE do nothing in the editor, and PLAY is Perform's. SONG closes the editor back to the SONG page, and BACK closes it.
- A sound swap or a new roster that replaces the part closes the editor, back to the Perform page, before the old Output is freed. Under a screen the editor opened (OSC 1's sample browser, say), the old Output waits until that screen closes back to the editor, which then closes. SOUND EDITOR does nothing while the CLIP page shows no part.

**MIDI in.**

- **MIDI-follow CCs** go where the gold knobs point: the focused part's Sound, or song-level params with nothing focused (F8). Only the follow channels A, B and C are followed: the per-track channels (Track 1–16) are off in Perform, so no part can be played, muted or soloed past the Generator. With nothing focused, a CC mapped to the song's tempo does nothing (no tempo CC, below).
- **External notes are dropped**, with their pitch bend, mod wheel, aftertouch and MPE, whether from MIDI follow or an Output's learned input (F18). They would be missing from the steering log and would bypass the note-off table and mute gate. A pitch bend or CC learned to a param still edits it.
- **MIDI-learned and MIDI-follow param edits** move homes like a knob turn and are logged. An incoming CC is read as a live value: home = incoming − current layer offset. LEARN stays stock, so a param learned inside Perform lives on that part's Output and is lost when the Output is replaced. MIDI follow is the route that lasts.
- **Macros over MIDI.** CCs 114–117 on the MIDI-follow channels steer Energy, Density, Complexity and Motion. 0–127 scales to 0–50. They set the Recipe centre, are logged, and get feedback. They win over a follow mapping of the same CC. While a KEYBOARD fader holds the macro, only the centre moves, as for a gold knob. With MIDI follow's DISPLAY PARAM on, they show the macro's popup.
- **Feedback** sends each followed param's live value (home plus all layers) and each macro's centre on the follow feedback channel. While playing, a pass at the stock automation-feedback rate sends each value that moved since it was last sent. "Disabled" means no passes, so only changes go out: a player edit of a param on what the gold knobs point at, as stock sends one, and a macro centre moved on the panel. A new context (focus, entry) sends every value again. Nothing else resends a value that hasn't moved, so a controller that drops a message, or is plugged in mid-set, shows a stale value until the next focus change or entry: the trade-off for fitting the link (H17, [§20](20-budgets.md#20-budgets-and-measured-facts)). A follow or macro CC's value isn't sent back on that CC; an edit through a learned CC goes out on its follow CC, which isn't a loop. Feedback goes out only on the follow channels A, B and C: set to a Track, it has no channel in Perform, which has no tracks. Stock re-sends only `isAutomated()` params, so F8 sends Perform's feedback from `View::sendMidiFollowFeedback` instead. With stock's feedback filter on, an incoming follow CC is dropped for a second after feedback went out on it (`timeLastCCSent`, `midi_follow.cpp`), so for a param a layer keeps moving, which is sent on every pass, incoming follow CCs are dropped while it moves.

**Global MIDI commands.**

| Command | In Perform |
|---|---|
| PLAY, FILL, SHIFT | as the panel button. PLAY starts with the Intro |
| RECORD | Capture mark, as the panel's RECORD ([§17.4](17-capture.md#174-marking)) |
| TAP | Recipe tempo, as below |
| TRANSPOSE | queues a Recipe key change to the next phrase line: the played note's pitch class becomes the key, the scale is kept. Stopped, it sets the key at once, as the key editor does. Note-offs do nothing |
| PLAYBACK_RESTART | blocked |
| LOOP, LOOP_CONTINUOUS_LAYERING, UNDO, REDO, NEXT_SONG | ignored. Perform has no undo |

While a Generative job's modal screen (entry, leave) is up, every global command's note-on is swallowed, so none reaches the song the job replaces.

**Tempo.**

- TAP sets the Recipe tempo through the tempo hook and glides at the Glide setting.
- No tempo CC. MIDI clock out stays stock and follows the gliding song tempo tick by tick.
- **Under an external clock** the master owns tempo and the glide is off. The Recipe tempo tracks the clock: sampled once per bar, rounded to a whole BPM, logged only when the rounded value changes. A tempo turn shows the stock tempo popup marked EXT and changes nothing. TAP is ignored. SHIFT+turn still steers swing, which still glides. X+turn nudges sync as stock. LEARN+turn is swallowed (F4), so the clock-out scale can't change inside Perform.
- When the clock stops being followed, the transport stops (stock), and the next PLAY starts at the last tracked tempo. A clock that stops with no MIDI Stop isn't lost to stock, which has no timeout: the transport keeps playing, the piece held where it was, until PLAY stops it.

### 13.8 Leaving

Holding BACK opens the leave menu, a stock context menu, while playback continues. The verb is in the title, so every option fits 13 characters. It starts on the first option.

| Situation | Title | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Marked pieces, last one unmarked | `KEEP: 2 MARKED` | MARKED + LAST, MARKED ONLY, NOTHING, CANCEL |
| The last piece is itself marked | `KEEP: 2 MARKED` | MARKED, NOTHING, CANCEL |
| No marks | `LEAVE, KEEP:` | LAST, NOTHING, CANCEL |
| No piece was played | `LEAVE PERFORM?` | LEAVE, CANCEL |

The counts and the last piece are the kept ones ([§17.7](17-capture.md#177-building-on-leave)): an earlier piece unmarked in CAPTURE ▸ isn't counted, nor is a piece with nothing logged. The option that keeps nothing (NOTHING, or LEAVE) is always the one before CANCEL.

- Any choice but CANCEL stops the transport.
- If pieces are kept, Capture builds and saves them ([§17.7](17-capture.md#177-building-on-leave)). The player lands on the last saved song in arranger view.
- If nothing is kept, the player lands on a new blank song in session view, the song SHIFT+LOAD → NEW makes. Leaving also empties the sample cache, the previous song's samples included ([§20](20-budgets.md)).
