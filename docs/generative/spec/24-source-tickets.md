- Tasks: [Build and run v1.3.0-rc3 in delugemu][t9], [Time a scripted capture in delugemu][t36], [Measure memory and load times on a Deluge][t37], [Hardware tests of the Perform POC][t45].
- Grilling: [Simulation approach][t8], [Recipe parameters][t10], [Genre model][t11], [Track roles][t12], [Sound generation][t13], [Automation][t14], [Perform interaction model][t15], [Compose output shape][t16], [Generator randomness and seeding][t17], [Generator scheduling][t19], [Sub-step expression][t20], [Pitch model][t21], [Porting policy][t23], [Kit lane mapping][t25], [Variation when a sound is picked][t27], [Parts that play some scale degrees][t32], [Capture][t35], [Entering Generative mode and the Compose screen][t39], [Recipe files][t40], [Testing and CI][t41], [Generative pack contents][t42], [Build order][t44], [External tempo and MIDI in Perform][t51], [POC gaps][t52], [Harmony for static genres and chord extensions][t64], [Fill kinds and phrase-end events][t65], [Dub's drop-outs and delay throws][t67], [Build shape and Section lengths][t68], [Interplay rule details and Register spans][t71], [Arc headroom, gestures per Section, and Motion][t72], [How a transition sounds between two genres][t69], [How Motion scales Groove length, fills and drop-outs][t81], [Transition lifecycle][t73].
- Prototypes: [Density as a fourth macro][t24], [Mapping steps onto 5- and 6-note scales][t29], [Pushed Lean][t30], [Chord graph across a genre's scales][t31], [Perform control layout][t38], [Compose screen and Generative menu prototype][t50], [Lean per 16th position and the flam][t70], [Held FILL and how a Rest ends sounding notes][t79].
