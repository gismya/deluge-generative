# 18. Generative pack

Part of the [Generative mode spec](../spec.md). Section numbers are shared across all files.

### 18.1 Shape

| On the card | Holds |
|---|---|
| `SYNTHS/GENERATIVE/` | our presets |
| `SAMPLES/FX/<lane>` | FX samples |
| `SAMPLES/DRUMS/<folder>` | `GEN`-prefixed one-shots in the factory folders, so the lane→folder table finds them |
| `GENERATIVE/` | `LICENSES.md`, the pack's origins and licences; `PALETTES/` is reserved for optional palette overrides |
| `RECIPES/` | created by the firmware |

- In the repo: `pack/`, laid out exactly as on the card. The harness reads it from there. WAVs go straight into git, with no LFS.
- `pack/LICENSES.md` lists each file's origin and licence. The pack's licences don't affect the firmware, so it may ship GPL-3.0-only data such as PERF-1's DX and DY voices (ADR 0001). The pack is CC0 1.0, except the presets derived from Synthstrom Audible's factory presets (below): they carry no CC0 claim, and `pack/LICENSES.md` lists each with the factory file it comes from and its edits, under **Derived from the factory presets**.
- Distribution: one zip attached to each firmware release, unzipped onto the card root. `scripts/generative/beta_release.py` makes it from `pack/` and puts `pack/LICENSES.md` at `GENERATIVE/LICENSES.md`, so the card root gets no stray file. It refuses to release a pack that `make_pack.py --check` (a file it doesn't write fails too) or `check_pack_velocity.py` fails, run on `pack/` as committed at the release's commit.
- Version skew between firmware and pack is accepted for now.

### 18.2 Contents

- **Presets in `SYNTHS/GENERATIVE/`:**
  - `FX/`: Riser, Fall, Impact and Siren presets. `GEN Siren` is a square and a saw with an LFO on pitch. The Impacts the owner picked by ear (#472, impact round 2; [§10.5](10-sounds.md#105-fx-lanes)): `GEN Impact Boom` (the factory `808 Kick sustained`) and Acid techno's `GEN Impact Boom Acid`, `GEN Impact Wash` (white noise and a quiet square and detuned saw a fifth over it through the high-pass and an env2-swept resonant low-pass, a 0.4 s splash into a big reverb and a dotted 8th analog ping-pong echo), `GEN Dub Impact Crack` (the factory DDD1 wet snare and 808 rim into a dotted 8th echo at feedback 20, which dies within seconds) and `GEN Impact KickCrash Dub` (the factory 808 kick and 808 cymbal layered). The three over factory samples are sample presets that play their files once through (ONCE), so the samples stay on the factory card and only the preset is in the pack. Each has its volume knob and sustain full, and plays about 6 dB over impact round 2's level, which the owner found too quiet, by a lever past the knob's top, measured in delugemu with the FX part alone: the boom's sample on both oscillators in phase (+6.0 dB), the wash's saturation (`clippingAmount` 4, +6.1 dB on its first beat), and the two Dub presets, whose oscillators are both taken, a velocity cable of 50 (velocity is bipolar about 64, so at an Impact's velocity of 80 to 94 it adds 5 to 6 dB). Saturation can't lift the sample presets: it caps a voice at a fixed level, under the boom's unsaturated peak. Impact round 3 (owner, #472) moved two: the boom 3 dB louder again (a velocity cable of 30, +3.0 dB peak alone), and the crack 3 dB quieter (the volume knob at 42, −3.1 dB); the wash and the kick and cymbal stay. At the boom's +3 dB Acid techno's Drop reached full scale (8 samples clipped, seed 1), so Acid plays its own copy at a cable of 22 (+2.0 dB), peaking at −0.1 dBFS; Detroit's Drop peaks at −3.6, Psytrance's at −1.9 and House's at −2.8 dBFS (delugemu, the pack card). No pack preset saves delay feedback of 25 or more: each repeat's gain is the value / 25, and from 25 an echo never dies in Perform ([§11.4](11-sound-controls.md#114-gestures)); `test_make_pack.py` checks every preset. The crack's first build saved 26 and its echo held at about −29 dBFS for good; at 20 it is under −50 dBFS 9 s after the hit. The wash's 24 decays: under −50 dBFS about 18 s after the hit.
  - A plain house chord stab with no built-in interval and no saved portamento.
  - A dub sub bass, a melodica, a tight psy bass (sustain 0) and a soft sine sub for Ambient.
  - Presets derived from factory ones (owner, #322): `GEN Echo Chord Dry`, `GEN Short Sharp Dry` and `GEN Kyoto Phono Dry`, Dub techno's Stab: 037 Echo Chord, 082 Short Sharp Delay and 072 Kyoto Phono with their own echo off (delay feedback 0) and louder, so the part's delay setup and its Throws alone make the echo ([§4.5](04-genres.md#45-v1-genres)). `GEN Dark FM Pad High`, Dub techno's Pad (owner, #120): 126 Dark FM Pad with both carriers and both modulators an octave up, so the FM ratios and the timbre stay.
  - `DX/`: only the PERF-1 DX/DY voices a palette uses, as synth presets with the patch inside (`dx7patch`). They are picked during tuning: likely EP stabs, basses and Ambient bells. They play even with the DX7 runtime setting off.
- **How it is made.** Every pack file, presets included, is generated by `scripts/generative/make_pack.py`: presets written as XML from code, samples synthesized. A preset close to a factory one is derived, never hand-edited: `make_pack.py`'s `DERIVED` table names the factory file (read from the factory card's files, checked by its SHA-256) and the edits (a field's value, a patch cable's amount, an oscillator's or modulator's transpose), and the script writes the result. `make_pack.py --check` fails while a derived file has no row in `pack/LICENSES.md`'s derived section; without the factory card's files (CI) it checks only that the derived files are there. A new derived preset is a `DERIVED` entry and a row there. Each genre's slice adds its own share there, and its palette names it; the player judges them by ear while tuning. Sounds that may be worth finding or recording by hand later are collected on their own ticket. Beta 2 needs every item below for the M2 genres; DX voices only if tuning picks one.
- **Samples**, all rendered from our own Deluge presets or generated by `scripts/generative/make_pack.py`, so we choose their licence: CC0 1.0 (`pack/LICENSES.md`):
  - At least 3 risers in `SAMPLES/FX/Riser`, trimmed to land on the file end, and `GEN Riser Short` (about 2 s, in `SAMPLES/FX/Riser/Short`, so only Dub's Riser lane reads it) for Dub's 1-bar Build, short enough for the same Build when it glides up to about 142 BPM on a change out of Dub ([§10.5](10-sounds.md#105-fx-lanes)'s Riser fit).
  - Two or more 909-style rides and claps, and a handful of soft one-shots for Ambient, named `GEN <kind>.wav`: brushes (`SAMPLES/DRUMS/Snare`), mallet thuds and inharmonic metal pings (`Other`) and wood hits (`Block`). Drums lanes are unpitched and never follow the key ([§5.4](05-parts-and-lanes.md#54-lanes)), so these are non-tonal: a tuned bell or mallet would clash in most keys. Ambient's pitched bells are its Lead's presets.
  - `GEN Sine Sub`, a soft sine bass (a sine with a slow attack, full sustain, a release of a second or two and a velocity cable), was Ambient's Bass until its sounds round 1; no palette lists it now.
  - Ambient's Drones (#313, sounds round 1; written field by field, borrowing only techniques from the Boards of Deluge pack): `GEN Ambient Drone 1` (tape strings: two detuned saws, three unison voices, a soft 12 dB low-pass, a breath of noise, a slow pitch wow), `GEN Ambient Drone 2` (a breathing filter: square and saw into a resonant low-pass a ~28 s LFO sweeps, per-voice pulse width) and `GEN Ambient Drone 3` (a glass fifth: a sine with a quiet sine at +19 a slow LFO fades). Each has a slow attack, a long release, the high-pass a little up, no oscillator under the note, and a velocity cable.
  - Ambient's Basses (same round): `GEN Ambient Bass 2` (felt pulse: two detuned squares, a 12 dB low-pass blooming over half a second), `GEN Ambient Bass 3` (folded sine: a sine and a quiet saw an octave up through the wavefolder, velocity folding more) and `GEN Ambient Bass 4` (tape bass: saw and triangle into a dark 24 dB low-pass with a filter bloom, hiss and a slow pitch wobble). Each has a soft attack, a release under a second, harmonics over the root for small speakers, and a velocity cable.
  - Dub's bass is `GEN Dub Sub`: a sine with a quiet triangle on it, a soft attack, sustain held while the note lasts and a quick release, and a velocity cable. Its melodica is `GEN Melodica`: a square reed and a saw detuned a few cents, a breath of noise, a soft attack, and velocity on volume and filter. `GEN Siren` is a square and a saw a fifth over it with a triangle LFO on pitch and a long release. `make_pack.py`'s `preset()` writes an unsynced LFO 1 for it.
  - House's chord stab is `GEN House Stab`: a plain saw and square at one pitch (no built-in interval, so a 7th or 9th voicing stays clean), no portamento, sustain 0 so each stab dies on its own, a filter pluck, and velocity on volume and filter.
  - Psytrance's tight bass is `GEN Psy Bass`: a saw and a square an octave under it into a resonant low-pass with a filter envelope, sustain 0 and a velocity cable. Its decay runs past the Loop's 20-tick gate, so the gate's release ends each 16th of the rolling bass.
- **Not in the pack:** a 303 acid preset (165 Acid Arp failed its ear check, and Acid techno's ACID plays 005 Sweet Mono Bass alone instead, #120), a psy drum set (tags reach `SAMPLES/Artists/Dean Daughters/Psy`), pack fall and impact samples, whole `.syx` banks, re-saved factory FM presets beyond the derived ones above, and palette override files.

### 18.3 Built-in palettes

- The v1 palettes start from the [draft palettes](https://github.com/gismya/deluge-generative/blob/research/factory-card-content/docs/research/factory-card-content.md#6-draft-palettes) in [Factory card content per role][t34], with these changes: pack entries where the drafts say "Pack"; Riser lanes list only pack risers; Dub gets a Siren lane and a factory boom added to its Impact lane; Ambient drops its Impact lane.
- Factory card facts: 103 subtractive, 54 FM and 14 ringmod synth presets, no wavetable or DX7. Bass, Pad and Lead are well covered and Chords thinly. Every drum lane has at least 3 samples. There's no `SAMPLES/FX`.
- Avoid presets with off-key tuning (040, 164 and 060 among 8). Presets with a built-in interval (30 of them) suit Bass, Lead and drones but not Chords.
- Every palette is starting data. A wrong pick is a data edit, and no ear check blocks implementation. The first check was 165 Acid Arp with its arp cleared: it sat too low and was dropped (#120).
- The pack grows genre by genre: each milestone ships the share its genres name ([§22](22-build-order.md#22-build-order-and-milestones)).
