Density as a fourth macro?

Today a Recipe has three macros. Energy moves two things at once: how many notes play (density) and how hard, bright and driven they are (intensity). This demo plays the same genres and parts both ways. With three macros, density and intensity rise together. With four, a separate Density macro owns the note count, so sparse but loud and busy but soft become reachable. The question is whether that extra knob earns its place on the Deluge, or whether one Energy knob is the better instrument. Press Play and use headphones. The synths are rough browser stand-ins for the Deluge's real sounds. Judge what the macros do, not the timbre.
