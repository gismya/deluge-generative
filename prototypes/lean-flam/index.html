Lean per 16th position, and which way round the flam goes

Lean moves a part's or lane's hits off the grid, −12 to +12 ticks (24 ticks per 16th, one tick is 625/BPM ms). The spec gives each lane two values: one for on-beat 16ths (the 1 and the &) and one for off-beat 16ths (the e and the a). So a lane can't move its & without moving its 1 as well. Question 1, by ear: do lanes need four values, one per 16th position (1, e, &, a), or are two enough?

A flam is a soft grace hit just before a main hit. The spec puts the grace hit at the Lean position and the main hit 1–3 ticks later, so the main hit is off the grid. Question 2, by ear: should the main hit sit at the Lean position with the grace hit earlier instead, and how far apart (in ticks, or in ms) do the two hits need to be to be heard as a flam?

The Generator decides each step 12 ticks ahead, so nothing may land more than 12 ticks early. On the first step of a Section, nothing may land before the line at all. Press Play and use headphones. The drums are rough browser stand-ins, but like samples, each one plays the same sound every time, so a short flam gap gives the same comb-filter smear it would on the Deluge.
