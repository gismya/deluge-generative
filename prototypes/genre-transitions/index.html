How a transition sounds between two genres

A genre change (also Regenerate and Recipe LOAD) currently plays the outgoing genre's Build and lands the new genre on a Drop. Tempo and swing glide over the whole Build, and the new tempo and key are plain rolls. This page plays both the Current spec rules and the Proposed ones so you can compare them by ear:
· Q1 tempo glides only for small changes and otherwise jumps at the Drop, inside the Gap if there is one;
· Q2 the new tempo roll leans towards the playing tempo ×½, ×1 or ×2;
· Q3 the new key roll leans towards keys that share 6 or 7 notes with the playing key;
· Q4 the incoming genre chooses how it's entered: Ambient enters by Fade through (outgoing Outro, then its own Intro);
· Q5 swing switches on the Drop or seam instead of gliding.

Start with the guided walkthroughs at the bottom. The sounds are rough browser stand-ins and the patterns are made-up starting points, so judge the shape of each transition, not the mix. Section lengths are the spec's genre values, so a Psytrance Build is 16 or 32 bars. Quick audition halves Builds, Intros and Outros. Headphones help.
