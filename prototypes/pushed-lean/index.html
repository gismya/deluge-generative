Pushed Lean: may a part play ahead of the grid?

Lean is a part's or lane's timing offset from the grid, set separately for on-beat 16ths (the 1 and the &) and off-beat 16ths (the e and the a), on top of song swing. Today it only goes later (0 to +12 ticks). This demo lets it go negative too, so a hat or snare can be pushed ahead of the grid. It asks two things. By ear: is a push worth having, and how far (−2, −4, −6, −12 ticks)? Does it sound any different from the late-only fake, where every other part plays late instead? By hand: to push by k ticks, the Generator has to decide each step k ticks before its grid line. Knob moves then reach the sound k ticks later, and a bar-quantised mute's window closes k ticks early. Can you feel that? The grid is 96 PPQN: 24 ticks per 16th, and one tick is 625/BPM ms. Press Play and use headphones. The synths are rough browser stand-ins, so judge the timing and not the sounds.
