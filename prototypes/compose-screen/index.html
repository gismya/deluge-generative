Firmware facts this prototype ran into

  • There is no stock unsaved-changes prompt. The firmware never tracks unsaved edits: LOAD replaces the song with no question, and SHIFT+LOAD asks only "Clear song?" with one option. The "normal unsaved-changes prompt" in the Entering Generative mode decision doesn't exist. The Confirm picker tries "none" (stock LOAD habit) and a one-option context menu modelled on "Clear song?".
  • A stock context menu is a 100×40 popup with two rows of 13 characters (78 px between x 23 and 101), and its top edge (y 4) is off the visible area. Longer options only side-scroll while selected. "KEEP MARKED + LAST" is 18 characters.
  • A stock submenu shows three rows of 21 characters at 9 px spacing (y 19, 28, 37), and a row with an icon stops at x 114. Submenus show no values and no scrollbar: values appear only inside an item.
  • Titles use the bold 9 px font with 2 px letter gaps and kerning, so a title holds about 15–18 characters. A horizontal menu's page counter takes the right 22 px, and its title has no separator line.
  • Horizontal menu column labels are cut to fit 32 px, which is four characters of the 6 px font. Stock items give short column labels ("FREQ", "RES").
  • The fonts have no "…" or "▸". Anything above "~" draws nothing, so FILE… is written FILE... and the ▸ is the submenu arrow icon.
  • A stock song load shows only the working animation: a 17×9 icon in the top-right corner over whatever was on screen. Stem export, the closest stock long job, shows "Exported 2 of 6 instruments" as popup-looking text, and BACK asks "Cancel Export?" before it stops.
