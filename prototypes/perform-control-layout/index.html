Shared controls (every pair)

Grid variants own the 16×8 main pads, the X turn, SONG, CLIP and KEYBOARD. OLED variants own the screen and the SELECT turn on the home screen. Everything else, including both sidebar columns, is shared. Blinking = queued, waiting for its bar or phrase line (it closes 12 ticks early). Pressing a queued action again, or tapping BACK, cancels it.
