PROTOTYPE, throwaway. Not the real firmware. Perform control layout, three variants. Right-click any pad or button to latch it held (for hold combos). Keyboard Shift = SHIFT, Space = PLAY, ←/→ = switch variant.

Pads count from the top-left: column 1–16, row 1–8, then the two sidebar columns (Mute = left, Audition = right). The OLED is capped at 4 rows of 21 characters, the real budget. Pad blink = action queued, waiting for its bar or phrase line (quantised actions close 12 ticks early, here half a 16th).
