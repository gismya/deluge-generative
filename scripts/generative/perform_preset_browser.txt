# Perform's preset browser (#299, spec §10.7, §13.5) over the sysex test port: SYNTH on a focused synth part opens it on
# the part's pick, scrolling loads nothing, BACK closes it with nothing changed, and a chosen preset (here one ACID's
# palette doesn't list) preloads and swaps in as a re-roll does: on the next phrase line while playing, at once while
# stopped. Its cancels, its refusals, the previous sound after it, NEW SOUND and a Regenerate after it, and SYNTH on a
# Kit part and KIT, which still re-roll. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu (test_port_emu.py).
# Start as after boot: transport stopped, the current clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Acid techno's parts: DRUMS row 1 (y 7), ACID row 2 (y 6), DRONE row 3, FX row 4. The QWERTY keyboard's digits are
# row y 5, '1' to '0' at x 3 to 12: typing "002" (x 12, 12, 4) jumps to SYNTHS/002 Basic Square Bass.XML.
# gen_browser open part= pick=<the part's pick> at=<the selected file>; gen_browser at=<folder>/<file> on each move;
# gen_browser chose part= path=; gen_browser closed. gen_press browser|chosen|sound part= previous= result=<0
# preloading|1 cancelled|2 busy|5 change pending> working=. gen_sound_press and gen_sound_preload end in chosen=<1 for
# the browser's preset>. ACID's palette: 165 Acid Arp, 015 Resonant Filter Bass, 097 Saturated Filter, 005 Sweet
# Mono Bass.
start_state
include perform_open.txt
include perform_confirm.txt

# ---- Lock ACID (double tap its row on the SONG page), and play ----
pad 9 6 down
wait 50
pad 9 6 up
wait 50
pad 9 6 down
wait 50
pad 9 6 up
expect event gen_focus part=1 locked=1
button CLIP_VIEW down
wait 50
button CLIP_VIEW up
expect event gen_page CLIP
button PLAY down
wait 50
button PLAY up
expect playing=true
expect event gen_section INTRO 0

# ---- SYNTH opens the browser on ACID's pick; scrolling loads nothing; BACK closes it with nothing changed ----
button SYNTH down
wait 50
button SYNTH up
expect event /gen_browser open part=1 pick=(SYNTHS\/.+\.XML) at=\1$/ within=10
expect ui=generative_preset_browser
expect root_ui=generative_perform
encoder 3 1
expect event gen_browser at=SYNTHS/ within=5
encoder 3 1
expect event gen_browser at=SYNTHS/ within=5
expect no_event gen_sound_press within=3
expect no_event gen_sound ACID within=1
expect playing=true
# Perform plays on under it: its frame runs, the sidebar's mute pad is live (a mute and its cancel)

wait 30
pad 16 6 up
expect event gen_press mute part=1 now=0
button BACK down
wait 50
button BACK up
expect event gen_browser closed within=5
expect ui=generative_perform
# BACK's release closed the browser, not a tap on Perform's queue
expect no_event gen_back_tap within=2
expect no_event gen_sound_press within=1
