# Perform's SONG page Arc map row 7 rule (#555, spec §13.5): row 7 shows effective Energy when the roster has 6 parts
# or fewer, and the 7th part when it has 7. No shipped genre has 7 parts yet (Goa, #458, adds the first), so this
# verifies a 6-part genre (Detroit techno): rows 1–6 (0–5) are parts, row 7 (index 6, y 1) is Energy (`e`) and does not
# focus a part on press, and row 8 (index 7, y 0) is the Section row. The 7-part row 7 case is covered in the harness
# (`arc_map_spec`, `sidebar_spec`, `roster_room_spec`).

expect ui_mode=0

button SHIFT down

wait 100
button SESSION_VIEW up
wait 100
button SHIFT up
expect ui=context_menu
expect root_ui=session
button 4 3 down

button 4 3 up
expect ui=sound_editor
encoder 3 5
encoder 3 -1

button 4 3 up

pick genre techno-detroit

button 4 3 down

button 4 3 up
wait 300
pick genre techno-detroit

button 4 3 down
wait 50
button 4 3 up
expect ui=context_menu
include perform_confirm.txt

# ---- SHIFT+PLAY: Groove from bar 1. Six parts on rows 0–5, Energy (`e`) on row 6 (spec row 7), Section on row 7 ----
mark
button SHIFT down
wait 50
button PLAY down
wait 50
button PLAY up
wait 50
button SHIFT up
expect playing=true
expect event gen_arcplan first=0 since=mark within=5
expect event gen_arcmap s=...GGGG since=mark
expect event gen_arcrow 0 ...np since=mark within=5
expect event gen_arcrow 6 ...eeeeeeeeeeeee since=mark within=5
expect event /^gen_sidebar mute=[ad]{6} / since=mark within=5

# ---- Row 6 (spec row 7, y 1) is Energy on a 6-part genre: pressing it does not focus a part ----
mark now
pad 9 1 down
wait 100
pad 9 1 up
expect no_event gen_focus since=mark within=1

# ---- Row 5 (spec row 6, y 2) is the 6th part (FX): pressing it focuses part 5 ----
pad 9 2 down
expect event gen_focus part=5 locked=-1 within=5
pad 9 2 up
expect event gen_focus part=-1 locked=-1 within=5

# ---- Stop and leave cleanly ----
button PLAY down
wait 50
button PLAY up
expect playing=false
include perform_leave.txt
expect event gen_perform_left within=10
