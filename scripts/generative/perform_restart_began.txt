# Restart AS IT BEGAN (#413, spec §13.2, §13.6, §17.2, §17.3) over the sysex test port: RESTART ▸ AS IT BEGAN starts
# the piece again from its header in the steering log. With nothing changed it queues to the next bar line at once;
# after steering (a macro, a take, a preset chosen in the preset browser, which ACID's palette doesn't list, so the pick
# surely changes) the part's first sound preloads under the working animation, then
# the restart queues, and on its line the first sound swaps back in, the param snapshot is written back, and the
# Current Recipe's macros and takes are the piece's first ones. The restarted run opens a piece of its own in the log.
# BACK cancels the preload. Stopped, it restores the last piece's start state, then starts. Runs on a Deluge
# (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu (test_port_emu.py). Start as after boot: transport stopped, the current
# clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Events: gen_restart_press began parts=0 | began_preload parts= stopped= submitted= | began_queued | began_cancelled:
# what AS IT BEGAN did (parts: a bit per part whose picks changed). gen_restart_preload_held parts=,
# gen_restart_preload done|cancelled|failed parts= error= missing= ms= working=: its preload. gen_restart_snapshot
# params=: params written back on the line. gen_restart as_it_began [stopped] tempo= energy=: it started the piece
# again (energy: the Energy macro's units). gen_log_piece pieces=: a piece opened in the log. Acid techno: ACID is part
# 1, its SONG row pad 9 6; perform_choose_002.txt chooses SYNTHS/002 for it. The Perform menu's rows with no transition to cancel:
# GENRE, REGENERATE, KEY / SCALE, GLIDE, SEED + TAKES, OCTAVES, RESET PART BIAS, RESTART; RESTART ▸'s: AS IT IS, AS IT
# BEGAN. Menus remember their row, so each choice counts from the top, and the script leaves both on their first row.
