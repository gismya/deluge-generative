# Shared by the Perform scripts (include perform_leave.txt) and start_state (deluge_probe.py runs it to leave
# Perform, stopping at the first failed expect): from Perform, stopped or playing, hold BACK for the leave menu and
# keep nothing (spec §13.8): NOTHING, or LEAVE before any piece, is always the option before CANCEL. One detent at a
# time, three to reach the last of at most four options: the context menu refuses a turn that would pass its end
# (a turn of 5 stays on the first option, LAST, which would start a Capture). Lands on a new blank song in session view, with no Capture.
button BACK down
expect ui=context_menu within=10
button BACK up
encoder 3 1
encoder 3 1
encoder 3 1
encoder 3 -1
button 4 3 down
wait 50
button 4 3 up
expect event gen_left within=15
expect root_ui=session within=10
expect ui_mode=0
expect playing=false
