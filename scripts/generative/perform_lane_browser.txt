# Perform's lane list and lane browser (#300 part 1, spec §10.1, §10.5, §10.7, §13.3, §13.5) over the sysex test port:
# KIT on a focused Drums or FX part opens the lane list (RE-ROLL ALL, then a row per lane); SELECT on a lane opens its
# browser on the lane's pick, where the sample under the cursor previews per BROWSER PREVIEW (WHEN STOPPED by default);
# BACK goes back to the list and BACK again closes it. A chosen sample preloads the whole Kit with only that lane
# changed (the other lanes load what they loaded at entry) and swaps in as a re-roll does; SHIFT + KIT brings the
# previous Kit back, RE-ROLL ALL re-rolls every lane, and KIT while the chosen sound waits for its line cancels it. An
# FX lane that takes a preset asks SAMPLE or SYNTH PRESET first, and a chosen preset loads into its row as a synth.
# BROWSER PREVIEW: OFF and ALWAYS from the Perform menu. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu
# (test_port_emu.py). Start as after boot: transport stopped, the current clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Acid techno's parts: DRUMS row 1 (y 7), ACID row 2, DRONE row 3, FX row 4 (y 4). The QWERTY keyboard's digits are row
# y 5, '1' to '0' at x 3 to 12. gen_lane_list part= rows=reroll,<lane>[+preset],...: the list opened (+preset: the lane
# asks SAMPLE or SYNTH PRESET). gen_browser open part= lane= kind=sample|preset at=<the selected file>;
# gen_browser at=<folder>/<file> on each move; gen_browser chose part= path= lane=; gen_browser closed.
# gen_browser_preview sounding=0|1 playing=0|1 path= (or "off playing="): the file under the cursor previewed, or not.
# gen_sound <part> <LANE> pick|fallback mode= prio= <path>: a Kit row loaded. The Perform menu's last row is BROWSER
# PREVIEW (encoder 3 20 reaches it whatever rows come before), gen_browser_preview_setting <0 off|1 when stopped|2
# always>.
