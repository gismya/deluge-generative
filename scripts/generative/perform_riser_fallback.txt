# The FX Kit's fallback Riser (spec §9.5, §10.5) over the sysex test port, on a card whose only pack Riser in
# SAMPLES/FX/Riser is the 8 s one and whose only shorter one is the 2 s GEN Riser Short. Stopped, REGENERATE narrows
# BUILD to HARD (4 or 8 bars), so the FX Kit loads the fallback beside the 8 s Riser; at 240 BPM or more even an 8-bar
# Build (8 s or less) is no longer than 8 s, so the forced Build plays the 2 s fallback, which starts late. At its note-on the
# fallback row's filters stand where the drawn row's do: the Riser lane's controls (its Rise's Brightness and Thin)
# move both rows. Perform's entry, the Regenerate's transition and Capture's song each load the same fallback, whatever
# the piece's Builds. Delugemu or a Deluge: on a Deluge the card needs the overlay's files (fixtures/
# perform_riser_fallback) and FineTempoKnob On, so a pushed tempo encoder turns in coarse steps.
#
# gen_riser_fallback loaded part= path=|none part=: the fallback an FX Kit loaded. gen_riser_on sample_ms=N at the
# Riser's note-on; gen_capture_begin, gen_capture_end: as in perform_capture_leave.txt; gen_riser_rows lpf=<drawn>/<fallback> hpf=<drawn>/<fallback>: both rows' LPF and HPF frequency
