# Shared by the Perform scripts (include perform_hold_back.txt): holds BACK until LEAVE PERFORM? is up, then lets go.
# The menu opens after the stock hold time (400 ms, LONG_PRESS_DURATION; delugemu reports 399-403). held_ms= is how
# long the press had been held then, on the firmware's audio clock: at least 350, and under a second, so a card access
# that delays the menu passes and a hold that regressed to seconds or to a tap fails. within=10 only waits out a slow host.
button BACK down
expect event /^gen_leave_menu open held_ms=(3[5-9]|[4-9]\d)\d$/ within=10
button BACK up
