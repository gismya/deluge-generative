# Punch-ins and stock Performance view (#301, spec §8.9, §13.5) over the sysex test port: the KEYBOARD page's
# punch-in pads (a Drums lane mute per lane on columns 9–10, HAT ROLL, SNARE ROLL, FLAM, DOUBLE KICK and PUMP on
# columns 15–16's top three rows) with the faders' 300 ms rule, then KEYBOARD again: stock Performance view's song FX
# pads hosted over Perform, with the sidebar still live, and KEYBOARD back. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session)
# or in delugemu (test_port_emu.py). Start as after boot.
#
# Pads: x 0–15 from the left, y 0 the bottom row (spec rows count from the top: row 1 is y 7). The Kick's mute is
# x 8 y 7 (Techno's Drums lanes: Kick, Snare, Rim, Closed hat, Open hat, Crash, Tom down column 9). HAT ROLL is x 14
# y 7, SNARE ROLL x 15 y 7, FLAM x 14 y 6, DOUBLE KICK x 15 y 6, PUMP x 14 y 5.
# gen_punch lanes=<a char per lane: m muted, M held, . playing> punch=<H S F D P on, lower case held, . off>: the
# pads. gen_punch_pad punch=<PunchIn> lane=<Lane> down|up on=0|1: a press or release and the pad's state after it.
# gen_punch_landed lanes=<hex laneBit()s> effects=<hex, bit per PunchIn> step=: what the core plays from that step.
# gen_pump on=0|1: the sidechain pump ducks the other parts or lets them go. gen_performance layout: stock's layout
# loaded. gen_performance_pad x= y= down|up value=<knob pos> held=0|1: a stock pad, held=1 once latched. gen_stutter
# on=0|1: stock's stutter started or ended, logged as SetStutter.
