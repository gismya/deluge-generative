# H13 (spec §20, §17.4) on hardware: marked 5-minute pieces of busy steering until the 128 KB steering log is full. Each
# piece starts, is marked at once (RECORD), plays 5 minutes of busy steering (capture_h13_minute.txt: a macro turn
# about every 16th) and stops. Once the log fills, the piece playing is cut and the next marks are refused (gen_mark

# doesn't run it). Run with --events 'gen_log|gen_mark|gen_capture|gen_record_led'.
