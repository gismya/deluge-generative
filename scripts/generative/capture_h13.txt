# H13 (spec §20, §17.4) on hardware: marked 5-minute pieces of busy steering until the 128 KB steering log is full. Each
# piece starts, is marked at once (RECORD), plays 5 minutes of busy steering (capture_h13_minute.txt: a macro turn
# about every 16th) and stops. Once the log fills, the piece playing is cut and the next marks are refused (gen_mark
# log_full, the LOG FULL popup). About 45 minutes: a measurement, not a check (no perform_ prefix, so check_all.py
# doesn't run it). Last, the worst move (spec §17.4): with the log full, the oldest piece unmarked in CAPTURE ▸, so
# the next start drops it and the steps after it move every later piece down, about 4 KB a step (gen_log_compact
# us=: each step's cost in the tick; gen_h1 and gen_h16 render_max_us around it). That start finds no room, so it
# logs nothing; the start after it opens a piece in the room the move freed.
# Run with --events 'gen_log|gen_mark|gen_capture|gen_record_led|gen_h1 |gen_h16'.
