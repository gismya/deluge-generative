# SPIKE (card-backed steering log, measurement only, not for merge; include only): about 5 minutes of Perform with
# busy steering and two genre changes into the heaviest genres. Acid techno (1 min of busy steering), BUILD INTO A
# DROP into Psytrance (the new piece's file and its 7 KB header land at the transition's start step), 1.5 min of
# busy steering, HARD CUT AT PHRASE into Detroit (6 parts), 1 min of busy steering, stop. The wrappers
# (spike_cardlog_*.txt) set the card log's holds first. Figures: --events 'gen_cardlog|gen_stream|gen_h1 |gen_h16|
# gen_transition start|gen_log_piece'.
