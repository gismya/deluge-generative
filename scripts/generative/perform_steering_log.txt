# The steering log (#143, spec §17.2, §17.3, H13 in §20) over the sysex test port: Perform's piece opens in the log
# at its first step with a header (the Recipe, the sound picks and the param snapshot), busy steering logs as it lands,
# a stop ends the piece, and the next start throws the unmarked piece away and opens a new one. Runs on a Deluge
# (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu (test_port_emu.py). Start as after boot: transport stopped, the current clip
# a synth or kit clip. Last, a REGENERATE by BUILD INTO A DROP: the incoming roster's piece opens at the start
# step.
#
# gen_log_piece pieces= header= used= full= moved= us=: a piece opened, its header's bytes, the bytes of marked pieces
# it moved down (spec §17.4) and what opening it cost in the tick. gen_log at=phrase|stop pieces= used= piece= header=
# records= end= cut= full=: the log's use at each phrase line and at a stop. Busy steering: the macros on F8 (Energy on
# the upper gold knob, Density on the lower), each turned a fast detent (4 knob steps) about every 16th, and the song's
# F1 filter knobs between them (song params, logged as SetParam).
# For figures: --events 'gen_log|gen_h1|gen_macro_landed'.
# covers: src/deluge/generative/core/steering_log.* src/deluge/generative/firmware/log_header.*

# covers: src/deluge/generative/firmware/entry_leave.cpp src/deluge/generative/perform/perform_driver.*
# covers: src/deluge/generative/firmware/sound_swaps.* src/deluge/generative/perform/sound_swap_lifecycle.h

# covers: src/deluge/generative/firmware/transitions.* src/deluge/generative/perform/transition_lifecycle.h
