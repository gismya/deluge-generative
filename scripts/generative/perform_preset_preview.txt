# Perform's preset preview (#443, spec §13.3, §13.5) over the sysex test port: stopped, with BROWSER PREVIEW at WHEN
# STOPPED (the default) or ALWAYS, the preset under the preset browser's cursor (and an FX lane's SYNTH PRESET browser's)
# loads into an Output of the part beside its own and plays one audition note; a move frees it before the next loads,
# and BACK leaves the part as it was: no sound press, swap, sound-control rebind or pick. A choice after previews (here
# while a preview still builds, under "hold preset_preview") preloads and swaps in as today, and SHIFT + SYNTH then
# brings back the pick from before, not a previewed one. A start frees a preview, and while playing presets don't
# preview, at ALWAYS too. OFF previews nothing. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu
# (test_port_emu.py). Start as after boot: transport stopped, the current clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Acid techno's parts: DRUMS row 1 (y 7), ACID row 2 (y 6), DRONE row 3, FX row 4 (y 4). The QWERTY keyboard's digits
# are row y 5, '1' to '0' at x 3 to 12. gen_preset_preview built|cancelled|failed part= error= ms= path=: a preview's
# build ended; held part=: a build waits under the hold; note part= lane=<lane or ->: its note on; note_off part=; freed
# part=: the Output freed. A preview's build reports no gen_sound events: those are the part's own loads.
# gen_browser_preview off playing=0|1: nothing previews. The Perform menu's last row is BROWSER PREVIEW (encoder 3 20
# reaches it), gen_browser_preview_setting <0 off|1 when stopped|2 always>.
# covers: src/deluge/generative/firmware/preset_preview.* src/deluge/generative/perform/preset_preview.h
