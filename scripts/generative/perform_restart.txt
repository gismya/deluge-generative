# Restart AS IT IS (#413, spec §13.2, §13.5, §13.6) over the sysex test port: X+PLAY while playing queues a restart to
# the next bar line (RESTART popup, RST in the status line), where the piece starts again at bar 1 with its Intro; BACK
# or a second X+PLAY cancels it; it is refused while a transition is pending, and a transition is refused while it
# waits; RESTART ▸ AS IT IS in the Perform menu does the same; stopped, X+PLAY is PLAY; under an external clock the
# clock keeps running and the piece restarts on the master's bar line. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session) or
# in delugemu (test_port_emu.py). The external-clock part sends MIDI clock from the host. Start as after boot:
# transport stopped, the current clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Events: gen_restart_press queued|cancelled|refused|stopped: what X+PLAY or AS IT IS did. gen_restart as_it_is
# tempo=: the restart reached its line and the piece started again. gen_section <SECTION> <n> clip_pos= bar=: a
# Section started; clip_pos=0 is on the stock clips' bar line. Menus remember their last row, so a row is chosen from
# the top (encoder 3 -20 first); with no transition to cancel, the Perform menu's rows are GENRE, REGENERATE,
# KEY / SCALE, GLIDE, SEED + TAKES, OCTAVES, RESET PART BIAS, RESTART.
# covers: src/deluge/generative/firmware/perform_adapter.* src/deluge/generative/perform/perform_driver.*
# covers: src/deluge/generative/perform/perform_steering.h src/deluge/generative/perform/queue_text.*
# covers: src/deluge/generative/core/steering.h src/deluge/generative/core/generator_steering.cpp
# covers: src/deluge/generative/perform/transition_lifecycle.h src/deluge/generative/perform/sound_swap_lifecycle.h
# covers: src/deluge/gui/menu_item/generative/perform_menu.* src/deluge/gui/views/perform_view.cpp
# covers: src/deluge/gui/views/perform_view_screen.cpp src/deluge/generative/firmware/output_sink.cpp
# covers: src/deluge/gui/context_menu/generative.cpp src/deluge/hid/buttons.cpp
# covers: src/deluge/gui/l10n/english.json src/deluge/gui/l10n/g_english.cpp
# covers: src/deluge/generative/core/genres/techno.cpp src/deluge/generative/core/types.h
# covers: src/deluge/generative/perform/oled_page.* src/deluge/generative/perform/text.*
