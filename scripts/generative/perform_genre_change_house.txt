# House's genre changes (#132, spec §9.7, §21.3) over the sysex test port, in one boot: Dub into House and back out in
# all three styles (Dub 70-80 BPM, House 120-126). Acid techno (the boot genre) cuts to Dub first. HOUSE is a GENRE
# row of its own (no parent), so picking it opens the Regenerate page at once, on DROP (its transition entry). The
# first change plays Dub's 1-bar Build, gliding about 75 -> 123 BPM in about 2 s, onto House's Drop; a forced Break in
# House keeps the Stab, the Strings and the closed hat sounding; the last change out of House plays its own 4- or 8-bar
# Build, gliding down to Dub's tempo onto Dub's Drop. Every start checks the glide arrived where the new roster starts:
# tempo_off (the song's tempo against the incoming Recipe's, in hundredths of a BPM) under 10 BPM; a glide that never
# ran is 40 BPM or more off. It is read at the start step's decision tick, 12 ticks early, where a fast glide is still
# a BPM or two short. A hard cut glides at the GLIDE setting (spec §13.3): the cuts' bound assumes its default, 1 bar,
# which it is after boot. Runs on a Deluge (deluge_probe.py session) or in delugemu (test_port_emu.py). Start as after
# boot: transport stopped, GLIDE at 1 bar, the current clip a synth or kit clip.
#
# Order: Acid -> Dub CUT, -> House BUILD (Dub's 1-bar Build), a Break in House, -> Dub CUT, -> House FADE (Dub's
# 1-phrase Outro), -> Dub FADE (House's 1-phrase Outro), -> House CUT, -> Dub BUILD (House's Build). Dub's phrases are
# 24-27 s, House's 15-16 s and its Builds 8-16 s.
# gen_confirm <genre> style=<0 drop|1 fade|2 cut> result=<0 started>; gen_outputs_freed ... total=N counts the outgoing
# roster's Outputs: Acid techno's 4, Dub's 5, House's 6.
# For figures: --events 'gen_confirm|gen_transition|gen_section|gen_h1|gen_h16|gen_outputs_freed'.
